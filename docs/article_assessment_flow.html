SciVer: high-level article assessment flow

This diagram summarizes how this repository evaluates article-grounded claims. The core pipeline expects SciVer-style claim records; the Attention example shows how one real PDF is converted into those inputs.

Key files: examples/attention_is_all_you_need/prepare.py, main.py, utils/input_processing.py, model_inference/*, acc_evaluation.py, examples/attention_is_all_you_need/report.py 
flowchart TD
    A["Article source: PDF or prebuilt SciVer dataset"] --> B{"Input type"}

    B -- "Real article demo" --> C["prepare.py: download PDF, extract text, crop visual evidence"]
    C --> D["paper.json: sections and captions"]
    C --> E["claims.json: claim records, labels, reasoning types, image paths"]

    B -- "Already benchmark formatted" --> F["data_path JSON: SciVer-style claim records"]

    D --> G["main.py loads queries"]
    E --> G
    F --> G

    G --> H["Select prompt by reasoning type: direct, parallel, sequential, analytical"]
    H --> I["utils/input_processing.py builds context, captions, and image attachments"]

    I --> J{"Model backend"}
    J --> J1["Pi RPC"]
    J --> J2["Azure GPT"]
    J --> J3["OpenAI-compatible Gemini"]
    J --> J4["Ollama, vLLM, or Laya"]

    J1 --> K["Model returns rationale plus final yes or no"]
    J2 --> K
    J3 --> K
    J4 --> K

    K --> L["outputs JSON: per-claim responses and metadata"]
    L --> M["acc_evaluation.py parses final verdict and compares with gold label"]
    M --> N["Accuracy by claim type and total accuracy"]

    L --> O["Optional report.py validates article-demo claims"]
    O --> P["SUMMARY, checks JSON, eval JSON"]

    P --> Q["Interpretation: SciVer verifies specific claims using article text plus visual evidence"]
  • What is assessed? Individual claims about a paper are classified as supported or unsupported.
  • Where multimodality enters: the model receives article text context plus one or two visual artifacts such as table crops or chart images.
  • Article-specific preparation: examples/attention_is_all_you_need/prepare.py converts one pinned PDF into runnable SciVer inputs.
